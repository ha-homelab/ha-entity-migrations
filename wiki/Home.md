# Home Assistant Entity Migrations Wiki

An offline, read-only planner for moving logical roles between physical channels.
This checked-in wiki is the versioned source. Hosted wiki publication is separate.

- [Русская главная](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-entity-migrations/wiki/Home-ru)
- [Installation and commands](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-entity-migrations/wiki/Installation)
- [Model and examples](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-entity-migrations/wiki/Model)
- [Troubleshooting and limitations](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-entity-migrations/wiki/Troubleshooting)
- [Project README](https://github.com/ha-homelab/ha-entity-migrations/blob/main/README.md)

All examples are synthetic. A ready result is bounded by supplied files and
explicit declarations. There is no apply command or connection to a real home.
